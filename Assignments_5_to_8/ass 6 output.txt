APPROACH COMPARISON
Brute force: O(2^N) time; tests every subset.
Dynamic programming: O(N * W) time and O(N * W) memory.
Greedy by utility/weight: usually O(N log N), but is not guaranteed optimal for 0/1 knapsack.
Perishable items are identified for planning; utility scores should include urgency to prioritize them.
For multiple trucks, solve as a multi-knapsack/assignment problem; for changing supplies, rerun DP after updates.
